# Human and AI-Assisted Contributions

## Human contribution

The human project owner provides the project direction, problem framing, product thesis, prioritization, review decisions, authorization boundaries, publication decisions, account/identity actions, and final submission authorization.

## AI assistance

AI assistance has been used for research support, architecture analysis, implementation and refactoring, test generation and execution, documentation drafting, release-gate design, security review, evidence organization, and demo/submission preparation. AI-generated or AI-modified technical work is subject to executable tests and human review rather than being treated as validated by generation alone.

## Pre-existing work and hackathon scope

The submission records the provenance of the VÉRTICE candidate and distinguishes work demonstrated by the exact submitted candidate from historical development artifacts. Third-party platform capabilities, including the Strands Agents SDK and its intervention/interrupt primitives, are not represented as original VÉRTICE inventions. The public candidate should disclose any material pre-existing VÉRTICE work required by the hackathon rules and should not attribute third-party functionality to the project team.

## Truthfulness policy

The project distinguishes **REAL**, **SIMULATED**, and **REPLAY** evidence. REAL_STRANDS is used only when the official SDK path has actually executed. No user counts, customers, production integrations, deployments, external effects, or legal/IP status are claimed without corresponding evidence.
